Temperanda ("we") makes software for iPhone, Ableton Live, and desktop audio hosts. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the products listed below.

## This website

This site is static and served by Cloudflare. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. Cloudflare may process standard connection data (such as IP addresses) to serve the site and protect it from abuse, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply and follow up. We do not share it with anyone else.
